Help & support

How do I book a parcel?

Go to Home → Send a new parcel, fill product, cities and weight, then Confirm booking. You get a booking number and the parcel appears in Orders and Tracking.

How do I track my parcel?

Open Orders, tap a parcel, and the Track screen shows the route, the courier and the delivery timeline.

How does the wallet work?

Your balance starts at $256.00. Each booking deducts its price. Top up or withdraw from the Wallet tab — everything is recorded in Transactions.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device only. There is no server: clearing the app data in Settings removes everything.
